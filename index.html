Tong Chen

I am a final-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Washington, advised by Luke Zettlemoyer and Hannaneh Hajishirzi. I have interned at Meta FAIR, AI2, Microsoft Research, and Tencent AI Lab. Before UW, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from the Yao Class at Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on how language models learn from experience:

  • Self-Improving Agents: Memory & Continual Learning. I build agents that turn past experience into reusable knowledge: meta-optimizing how agents save and load memory, training note-taking models with reinforcement learning across streams of tasks, compiling context directly into model weights, and learning retrievers that surface the right knowledge at the right granularity.
    NoteTaker (preprint) · Generative Adapter (ICLR'25) · Revela (ICLR'26) · Dense X Retrieval (EMNLP'24)
  • RL Post-Training & Alignment. I study how to post-train language models to be factual and trustworthy without losing their other capabilities: reinforcement learning with binary retrieval-augmented rewards that reduces hallucination, preference optimization against verbatim regurgitation of pre-training data, and benchmarks for literal and non-literal copying in long-form generation.
    Binary RAR (ICML'26 Spotlight) · ParaPO (COLM'25) · CopyBench (EMNLP'24)
