Tong Chen

// Ph.D. Candidate · University of Washington

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Washington, advised by Luke Zettlemoyer and Hannaneh Hajishirzi. I have interned at Meta FAIR, AI2, Microsoft Research, and Tencent AI Lab. Before UW, I studied in the Yao Class at Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on how language models learn from experience:

  • Self-Improving Agents: Memory & Continual Learning. I build agents that turn past experience into reusable knowledge: meta-optimizing how agents save and load memory, training note-taking models with reinforcement learning across streams of tasks, compiling context directly into model weights, and retrieving knowledge at the right granularity.
    Agent Meta-Optimization (FAIR'26) · NoteTaker (preprint) · Generative Adapter (ICLR'25) · Dense X Retrieval (EMNLP'24) TODO: FAIR paper short name, disclosure
  • RL Post-Training & Reward Design. I study how models learn to game the rewards they are trained on, and how to design rewards that resist it: binary retrieval-augmented rewards that reduce hallucination without sacrificing other skills, preference optimization against verbatim regurgitation of pre-training data, and benchmarks for literal and non-literal copying in long-form generation.
    Binary RAR (ICML'26) · ParaPO (COLM'25) · CopyBench (EMNLP'24) TODO: ICML Oral or Spotlight?
